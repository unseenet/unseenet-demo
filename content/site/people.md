# Who lives here

Five made-up people, each with a profile, a picture, a personal feed and a site of
their own. Open their profile from any of their posts - their feed and site are
there, to read without following them.

- 🐹 **Mira Holm** - two guinea pigs and opinions about hay.
  Feed `{feed:mira}`, site `{site:mira}`
- 🐝 **Tomas Berg** - six hives behind the old school.
  Feed `{feed:tomas}`, site `{site:tomas}`
- 🍞 **Nadia Ruiz** - bread before sunrise, a starter called Gordon.
  Feed `{feed:nadia}`, site `{site:nadia}`
- 📻 **Ole K.** - antennas, old keyboards, contacts far away.
  Feed `{feed:ole}`, site `{site:ole}`
- 🌌 **Ines Varga** - trails, lakes and the Milky Way.
  Feed `{feed:ines}`, site `{site:ines}`

To follow a person: `{person:mira}` is Mira, for example. Following a person follows
their personal feed and site, and any feed they list in their profile as theirs.
