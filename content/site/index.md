# Welcome to the Unseenet demo

This is a **site** in [Unseenet](https://unseenet.org): a few pages that stay, unlike
posts, which come and go with their group. You are reading it in your own client,
fetched over Tor from a node - there is no web server behind it.

{picture:trail}

## What is here

- [What Unseenet is](about) - in a few minutes.
- [Try it](try) - things to do in the demo, from following someone to posting a picture.
- [Who lives here](people) - the five (made-up) people of the demo and their feeds.
- [Credits](credits) - where the pictures come from.

## The groups
