# What Unseenet is

**Usenet, unseen.** Unseenet is a discussion network in the spirit of old Usenet and
the BBSes: groups, threads, chat and feeds on nodes anyone can run, linked over Tor
onion services.

- **No company in the middle.** Nodes are run by people and organisations; they pass
  posts to each other, as Usenet servers did. Anyone can run one, with no public IP.
- **Signed, and encrypted where it should be.** Every post is signed by its author's
  key. Closed groups and private posts are end-to-end encrypted, so nodes carry only
  what they cannot read.
- **Hard to see who is talking.** Everything goes over Tor, nodes forget the route a
  post took, and pictures lose their metadata before they leave your machine.
- **Yours to keep.** Your keys are in an encrypted vault - on your computer, or on a
  USB stick you carry.
- **Written down.** The protocol is a strict profile of NNTP, described in numbered
  documents, so anyone can write a node or a client.

It is pre-alpha and has not had a security review yet: a project to play with and
pull apart, not one to protect anybody with yet.
