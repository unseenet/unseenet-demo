# Things to try

1. **Follow someone.** Pick a person from [Who lives here](people) and follow them:
   their posts come into your timeline. They are not told - who you follow stays in
   your vault.
2. **Comment** on a post in [Around the village]({address:feed}). Comments are open.
3. **Say hello** in the chat, `{address:chat}`, or start a thread in the discussion
   group, `{address:discuss}`.
4. **Post a picture** to the photos group. Your client takes out where and when it
   was taken and what took it - check: nothing of it reaches the network.
5. **Look at a pinned post.** The first post in the feed and in the discussion group
   is pinned: it stays at the top, and stays after the group's other posts expire.
6. **Make a site of your own.** Every identity can start a personal site and write
   pages like these.

On the command line, the same things are:

```
unseenet follow <fingerprint or name>
unseenet timeline
unseenet post <group> --reply-to <message-id> --body "Hello!"
unseenet post <group> -s "My picture" --attach photo.jpg
unseenet site-start
```

*Everything you do here is reset on Monday.*
