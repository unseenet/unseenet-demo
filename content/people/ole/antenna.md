# A wire antenna for beginners

A half-wave dipole: two lengths of wire, a little coax, and something tall to hang it
from.

## Length

Each leg is a quarter of the wavelength. In metres, roughly **71 ÷ the frequency in
MHz** - for 14.2 MHz that is about 5 metres a leg. Cut them a bit long and trim.

## Up it goes

As high and as clear as you can, away from house wiring. A tree works; so does a
fishing pole.

## Before you transmit

You need a licence, and you need to check the match (SWR) before you put real power
into it. Ask your local radio club - they love this question.
