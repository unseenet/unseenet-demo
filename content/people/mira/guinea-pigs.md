# Looking after guinea pigs

Short notes from four years with Pepper and Biscuit. Not a vet's advice - ask one.

## Never alone

Guinea pigs are herd animals. Two is the smallest herd; one is a lonely guinea pig.

## Hay, hay, hay

Fresh hay all day is most of what they eat - it wears their teeth down, which never
stop growing. Then a handful of greens, and pellets only as a small extra.

## Vitamin C

They cannot make it themselves. Pepper loves a piece of red pepper (yes, really);
Biscuit prefers parsley.

## Room to run

More floor than you think, and somewhere to hide. Mine "popcorn" - jump straight up
for joy - when they get the hallway in the evening.
