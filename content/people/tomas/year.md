# A beekeeper's year

Roughly how it goes here; your bees will have their own calendar.

| When | The bees | Me |
|---|---|---|
| Winter | a tight cluster, keeping the queen warm | leave them alone, mend frames |
| Early spring | the first pollen, the colony grows | the first look inside, on a warm day |
| Late spring | swarming season | check for queen cells every week |
| Summer | the main flow - nectar everywhere | add boxes, take the first honey |
| Late summer | the colony shrinks | the last harvest, treat against mites |
| Autumn | robbing, wasps, getting ready | feed if they need it, narrow the entrances |

## The one rule

Calm movements, no perfume, and never stand in front of the entrance - that is their
runway.
