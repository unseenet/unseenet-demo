# Finding the Milky Way

## When

A night without a moon, or with the moon set. In the north, late summer and early
autumn show the brightest part, low in the south after dark.

## Where

As far from town lights as you can get - an hour's drive often makes all the
difference. Coasts and hills are good: nothing between you and the horizon.

## How

- Give your eyes 20 minutes in the dark. No phone screen - or a red one.
- Look a little to the side of what you want to see; the edge of your eye sees faint
  light better.
- Bring something warm, even in August.
