# Gordon's bread

One loaf, at home, without a bakery's oven.

## What you need

- 100 g active sourdough starter (fed 6-8 hours before)
- 350 g water
- 500 g bread flour
- 10 g salt

## How

1. Mix the starter and the water, then the flour. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Add the salt with a splash of water and squeeze it in.
3. Over the next two hours, stretch and fold four times.
4. Let it rise until it is about half as big again - in a warm kitchen 4 hours, in a
   cold one longer. Watch the dough, not the clock.
5. Shape, put it in a floured basket, and into the fridge overnight.
6. Bake in a lidded pot at 250 °C: 20 minutes with the lid, 20-25 without.
7. Wait an hour before you cut it. Yes, a whole hour.
